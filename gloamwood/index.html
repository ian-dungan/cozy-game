How to Play x
Move W/S or arrows. Turn A/D. Strafe Q/E. Jump Space. Autorun R. Drag the mouse to look.
Target click a creature, or Tab for the nearest. Esc clears it.
C consider your target first. Its colour tells you how dangerous it is.
F toggles auto-attack. 1-8 use your spell and ability slots.
X sit to recover health and mana much faster. Standing or moving interrupts it.
Right-click a corpse to loot it, or a townsperson to talk and trade. H hails your target.
Casters buy scrolls from their guild in Lanternhold and click them to scribe. Moving while casting interrupts the spell.
If you die past level 4 you lose experience and your bags stay on your corpse. Run back and right-click it.
Talking press Enter and type to speak to those nearby. /shout reaches the zone, /ooc reaches everyone, /tell Name message is private.
Groups target a player and type /invite (or /invite Name). They type /accept. Groups share experience; /g talks to the group, /disband leaves.
Heals and blessings land on a player you have targeted, otherwise on yourself.
Other commands: /who /sit /stand /con /loc /time /pet attack /pet back /pet dismiss
